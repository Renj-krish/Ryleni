01. Scoping, Estimations & Costs

3 Questions

Every development project at Ryleni is scoped transparently based on architectural specifications, required third-party integrations, and performance SLAs:

  • Rapid MVPs & Focused Web Applications: Typically range from $5,000 to $15,000, designed to validate market viability within 4–8 weeks.
  • Custom SaaS Platforms & Mobile Products: Typically range from $20,000 to $60,000, featuring robust microservices, payment gateways, role-based dashboards, and automated testing.
  • Enterprise-Scale Systems & AI Integrations: Range from $75,000+, incorporating multi-tenant architectures, custom machine learning models, legacy database refactoring, and high-concurrency cloud scaling.

We operate with transparent milestone schedules and fixed-price contracts or Agile sprint retainers with zero hidden charges. Learn more on our Custom Software Development page.

Our Agile delivery model ensures predictable velocity and continuous feedback across structured phases:

  • Discovery & Architectural Blueprint (Weeks 1–2): Requirement decomposition, technical feasibility study, database schema design, and sprint roadmap planning.
  • UI/UX Design & Interactive Prototyping (Weeks 2–4): Complete wireframes, Figma design systems, and clickable user testing prototypes.
  • Core Engineering & Sprints (Weeks 4–12): Incremental feature implementation with bi-weekly client demos and staging deployments.
  • Quality Assurance, Penetration Testing & Launch (Weeks 12–14): End-to-end automated testing, load testing, security audits, and production rollout.

Most custom web projects launch in 6 to 10 weeks, while comprehensive enterprise suites span 3 to 6 months.

We offer flexible, client-first contractual structures aligned with your risk tolerance and operational needs:

  • Fixed-Scope Milestone Delivery: Ideal when technical requirements and design assets are clearly specified upfront. Pricing and delivery dates are guaranteed.
  • Dedicated Engineering Pods (Team Augmentation): Full-time senior engineers, cloud architects, and QA specialists integrated seamlessly into your existing engineering workflows.
  • Product Foundry Retainer: Monthly sprint allocation covering continuous product iteration, feature acceleration, DevOps support, and UI/UX optimization.
